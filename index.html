About the Central Bank of Kenya

The Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) is Kenya's central bank. Its principal objective is to formulate and implement monetary policy directed at achieving and maintaining price stability, supporting the stability of the Kenya shilling.

Read CBK's official Monetary Policy overview.
